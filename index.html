Predicted probability of prolonged LOS

Complete all six inputs and select Calculate probability.

Risk groups
  • Low risk: predicted probability <10%.
  • Intermediate risk: predicted probability 10% to <39%.
  • High risk: predicted probability ≥39%.

Risk groups use the unrounded probability. The displayed percentage is rounded to one decimal place.

About the model

The predicted probability is calculated using a multivariable logistic regression model incorporating pulse rate, Killip class, EF, NT-proBNP, D-dimer, and CCU admission within the first 48 hours.

NT-proBNP and D-dimer are entered in their original units and transformed using the natural logarithm for calculation.

This calculator was developed for research purposes and is intended to support, not replace, clinical judgment.

The model was developed from a single-center cohort and has undergone internal holdout validation; external validation is required before broader clinical implementation.
